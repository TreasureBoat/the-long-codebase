If you have a system that is old, load-bearing, and understood by fewer people every year,
that is the work we do.

## What we take on

**An assessment.** You need to know what is actually in a system before you commit to
anything — what modernizing it would cost, what would break, what nobody has looked at in a
decade. Fixed scope, and the report is yours whether or not we do anything else. Most
engagements should start here, because most plans we are asked about are built on a guess
about the current state.

**Reverse engineering something undocumented.** Nobody currently understands it. The people
who wrote it have gone. You need the rules it actually enforces written down before you can
replace it, integrate with it, or safely change it. Paul did this by hand for years before
there were tools for it — Maine's state budget system, and the US Navy's ammunition system
off COBOL and IDMS.

**A conversion or migration that cannot fail.** Database conversions, framework and language
upgrades, cutovers with a fixed window and paying customers on the other side. The method is
rehearsal: run it, count what changed, run it again, until the count stops surprising you.

**Getting AI into a team that distrusts it.** Often the team is right to. We have both
been on that side — Ken stopped using an assistant outright after it handed him a country
list that was quietly missing dozens of entries, and Paul managed engineers using it for
years without writing a line with it himself. What we can offer is the structure that makes
it safe: where it earns its keep, where it will confidently tell you something false, and
how to catch that before it reaches production.

**Talks and workshops.** We write this up because there is not much honest writing about it.
We will also come and say it out loud.

## What we can honestly commit to

Neither of us is idle. Ken maintains a framework and the servers it runs on; Paul has a
full-time engineering job. We would rather tell you that than take the work and be slow.

In practice that makes assessments, advisory and fixed-scope pieces the things we can do
well and on time. Larger hands-on delivery is possible, and we will tell you plainly what we
can staff before you plan around it.
